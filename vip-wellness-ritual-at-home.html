FAQ

VIP Wellness Ritual: Questions

What is the VIP Wellness Ritual?
An in-home session that combines guided Tai Chi breathing and gentle movement, certified manual lymphatic drainage, and focused neck and shoulder massage. It is led personally by HyFlow owner and lead therapist Tina Zhang and is offered only at your home, hotel or office.
How much does it cost?
The VIP Wellness Ritual is $500 for 120 minutes or $600 for 150 minutes. Couples VIP (two people, two therapists) is $950 for 120 minutes or $1,150 for 150 minutes. 15- and 20-visit packages are available. Travel is included within about 60 km of Mountain View; extended areas add $50 or $80 per visit.
Do I need Tai Chi experience?
No. The Tai Chi portion is gentle breathing and slow movement, adapted to your comfort and mobility, and you can do it in everyday comfortable clothing.
Why combine Tai Chi with lymphatic drainage?
Slow, deep breathing and gentle movement help you relax and support natural circulation, which pairs well with manual lymphatic drainage. The ritual reflects Tina's family background in traditional Chinese medicine combined with modern certified MLD.
Can we book it as a couple?
Yes. Couples VIP starts with Tai Chi together, then two therapists provide lymphatic drainage and neck and shoulder massage side by side.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve the Bay Area from our Mountain View studio, including Mountain View, Palo Alto, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Los Altos, Santa Clara, San Jose, Saratoga, Campbell, Los Gatos, Menlo Park, Atherton, Redwood City, San Mateo, Fremont, San Francisco, Santa Cruz and nearby cities — travel included. Extended areas within about 2 hours have a travel fee per visit: +$50 for 60–100 km (Oakland, Berkeley, Walnut Creek, Pleasanton, Half Moon Bay, Gilroy) and +$80 for 100–160 km (Carmel, Monterey, Marin, Napa).
