FAQ for HR, office and finance teams

Corporate Massage: Common Questions

How much does corporate massage cost?
All corporate programs use one rate: $150 per therapist hour with a 4-hour minimum per booking ($600), which covers about 14–16 employees at 15 minutes each. 20 employees take about 5.5 hours, or roughly $825. Monthly programs start at $1,200 per month and annual contracts at $9,000 per year.
Can we start with a pilot event?
Yes. Many companies start with a single 4-hour chair massage event ($600) with no contract, then move to a monthly program if the team enjoys it.
Can you provide a certificate of insurance (COI) for vendor approval?
Yes. We provide a certificate of insurance on request and can complete your company's standard vendor setup forms — mention it in your inquiry and we will send what you need with your quote.
How do invoicing and payment work?
We invoice your company directly. You can pay by corporate card or ACH, and Net-30 payment terms are available.
What do you need from us on the day?
A quiet corner or small room about 6 × 8 ft, close to a power outlet if possible, and a sign-up list. We bring the massage chair and all supplies; employees stay fully clothed and go straight back to work.
Are gift card amounts the same as corporate prices?
No. Gift cards are a separate product. Corporate pricing is $150 per therapist hour with a 4-hour minimum ($600), monthly programs from $1,200 per month and annual contracts from $9,000 per year.
