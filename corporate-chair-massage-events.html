FAQ

Events & Occasions: Questions

What events is chair massage good for?
Employee appreciation days, holiday parties, wellness weeks, health fairs, open enrollment, conferences, trade shows, offsites and busy-season breaks.
How much does chair massage for a corporate event cost?
$150 per therapist hour with a 4-hour minimum: $600–$1,050 for one therapist (4–7 hours), $1,200–$2,100 for two and $1,800–$3,150 for three.
Can you do chair massage at a conference or trade show booth?
Yes. We can staff a booth or lounge with one to three therapists; for more, text (408) 668-6562. Let us know the venue rules for load-in and power.
When is Employee Appreciation Day?
Employee Appreciation Day is the first Friday in March. It is a popular date for chair massage, along with December holiday events.
