FAQ

Mobile Massage in San Jose: Questions

How much does mobile massage cost in San Jose?
Mobile massage in San Jose is $300 for 90 minutes or $400 for 120 minutes, plus a $80 travel fee (San Jose is in our Zone 2). Post-op lymphatic drainage at home is $375 (90 min) or $500 (120 min) plus travel, and the Mother & Baby Bonding Package is $400 for 2 hours plus travel. Prepaid 10-session packages include travel: $2,500 (10 × 90 min) or $3,400 (10 × 120 min).
Do you offer lymphatic drainage at home in San Jose?
Yes. In-home manual lymphatic drainage (MLD) is our signature mobile service. Sessions are performed by Tina Zhang, a California Certified Massage Therapist trained in the Vodder method of MLD through Klose Training. For post-surgical clients (BBL, liposuction, Lipo 360, tummy tuck, mommy makeover, breast surgery, facelift and more), we require written clearance from your surgeon and follow your surgeon's post-op timeline. Many liposuction clients benefit from a series of 15–20 sessions.
What is the Mother & Baby Bonding Package?
A 2-hour session for a new mom and her baby: 90 minutes of postpartum massage or lymphatic drainage for mom, followed by 30 minutes of gentle infant massage. The infant portion is therapist-guided — Tina works with your baby while showing you simple, soothing techniques you can continue at home. It is $400 in our studio, or $400 plus the travel fee at home. We recommend checking with your pediatrician before your baby's first massage.
What do I need to prepare for an in-home massage?
Just a quiet space about 8 × 10 feet (2.5 × 3 m) where a massage table can be set up, ideally in a warm room. We bring the professional table, fresh linens, pillows and bolsters, oils and lotions. Please let us know about parking, gate codes and pets in advance. Setup takes about 10–15 minutes and is not taken from your session time.
Is there a minimum session length for mobile massage?
Yes. Mobile sessions are a minimum of 90 minutes, and we ask for at least 24 hours' notice. Couples or family members at the same address can book back-to-back sessions with a single travel fee.
Can I have a mobile massage at a hotel or office?
Yes. We come to homes, apartments, hotels and offices. For workplace events and recurring employee massage, see our corporate wellness programs.
Which languages do you speak?
Sessions can be conducted in English, Mandarin Chinese (中文) or Russian (Русский).
Can I use HSA or FSA for mobile massage?
Massage therapy can be an eligible HSA/FSA expense when it treats a specific medical condition, usually with a Letter of Medical Necessity from your doctor. We also provide superbills for out-of-network insurance reimbursement on request. Reimbursement is not guaranteed.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please give 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may be charged 50% of the service fee, and no-shows are charged in full.
