In-Home Massage for Tech Professionals:
Tech Neck, Desk Back & the VIP Wellness Ritual

By Tina Zhang, CMT  ·  November 2026  ·  6 min read

Ten hours at a monitor, back-to-back video calls, a laptop on the couch at night — Silicon Valley work leaves a recognisable pattern in the body: head forward, shoulders rounded, upper back locked, hips tight, lower back tired. A massage helps, but driving to a spa after work and sitting in traffic again undoes much of it. So we come to you — at home, before or after your workday, or between meetings.

The Short Answer

Our Tech Professional Reset is focused deep tissue and therapeutic work at home for $300 / 90 min or $400 / 120 min. The VIP Wellness Ritual combines guided Tai Chi, manual lymphatic drainage and neck & shoulder release for $500 / 120 min or $600 / 150 min, in-home only. Travel is included within about 60 km of Mountain View.

The Desk-Worker Pattern We See Most

  • Tech neck — tight muscles at the base of the skull and side of the neck, often with tension headaches
  • Upper back and shoulders — the area between the shoulder blades, and the front of the chest from rounding forward
  • Forearms and hands — from typing and mouse work
  • Hips and lower back — from sitting most of the day
  • Mental load — poor sleep and a nervous system that never quite switches off

Option 1 · Tech Professional Reset

A focused session built around the pattern above: deep tissue and trigger-point work on the neck, shoulders and upper back, release for the chest and forearms, then hips and lower back. Pressure is adjusted to you; cupping or hot stone can be added for +$30. Ideal as a regular maintenance session every one to two weeks.

A Note on Pain

Massage can ease muscle tension, but numbness, tingling or weakness in the arms or hands, or pain that is getting worse, should be checked by a doctor or physical therapist. Massage therapy may be an eligible HSA/FSA expense with a Letter of Medical Necessity.
