Post-Surgery Recovery

Post-Op Lymphatic Drainage at Home:
Recovering From Lipo, BBL or Tummy Tuck Without Leaving the House

By Tina Zhang, CMT  ·  November 2026  ·  7 min read

The first two weeks after liposuction, a BBL or a tummy tuck are when lymphatic drainage helps the most — and when getting to an appointment is hardest. You may be sore, wearing a compression garment, unable to sit after a BBL, or not cleared to drive. Post-op lymphatic drainage at home removes all of that: your therapist comes to your home, recovery house or hotel, and you stay put afterwards.

The Short Answer

HyFlow Massage LLC provides post-surgical manual lymphatic drainage at home for $400 per 90 minutes or $600 per 120 minutes, with travel included within about 60 km of Mountain View. Most clients start once their surgeon gives written clearance and book a series of about 12–20 sessions over 6–8 weeks.

Why Recover at Home?

  • No driving, no sitting in the car. Especially important after a BBL, when sitting is restricted, and after a tummy tuck, when you are still bent forward.
  • The benefit lasts longer. You stay lying down and calm after your session instead of walking to a car park and sitting in traffic.
  • Frequent sessions are realistic. Two or three sessions a week in the first month is much easier when the appointment comes to you.
  • Privacy. Many clients prefer not to be seen in the early swollen and bruised stage.
  • Recovery houses and hotels welcome. If you travelled to the Bay Area for surgery, we come to where you are staying.

When Can You Start?

Always after your surgeon says yes, in writing. Many surgeons clear liposuction and BBL clients within the first week; tummy tuck and combined procedures are often a little later. We follow your surgeon's timeline and instructions about drains, garments and positioning. Read more in What to Expect from Lymphatic Drainage After Surgery.

A Typical Home-Visit Schedule

Weeks 1–4
2–3 home visits a week. Swelling is highest; this is when sessions make the biggest difference to comfort and help prevent hard, lumpy areas.
Weeks 5–8
1–2 visits a week. Tissue softens; work focuses on firm or uneven spots. Many clients switch to our Mountain View studio here, where sessions cost less.
Month 3+
As needed. Occasional sessions for lingering firmness.

This is a general pattern, not a prescription — see How Many Lymphatic Drainage Sessions After Lipo or BBL for procedure-by-procedure ranges.

What to Have Ready

  • Written clearance from your surgeon — required before the first session
  • Your surgery date and procedures, and any instructions about positioning (for example, no pressure on BBL grafts)
  • Your compression garment and foam boards, so you can put them back on right after
  • An old towel or two, a warm room and space for a massage table near a bathroom
  • A friend or family member at home for your first visits if you are still unsteady on your feet

Who Performs Your Sessions

Post-op sessions are led by Tina Zhang, a California Certified Massage Therapist trained in the Vodder method of MLD through Klose Training, or a California-licensed therapist on her team trained in post-surgical lymphatic drainage. Post-op sessions are usually with a female therapist unless you request otherwise.

Call Your Surgeon First

Lymphatic drainage supports recovery but does not replace medical care. Contact your surgeon right away for fever, spreading redness or heat, drainage from an incision, sudden swelling or pain in one leg, or worsening pain. Call 911 for chest pain or trouble breathing.
