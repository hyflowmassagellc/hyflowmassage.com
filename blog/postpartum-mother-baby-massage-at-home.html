Mother & Baby

Postpartum Massage at Home:
Mother & Baby Bonding Massage for New Families

By Tina Zhang, CMT  ·  November 2026  ·  6 min read

In the weeks after birth, leaving the house is a project: feeding schedules, car seats, naps, and a body that is still healing. Yet this is exactly when many new mothers carry the most tension — sore shoulders and upper back from feeding and carrying, a tired lower back, and swelling in the legs and feet. Our Mother & Baby Bonding Package brings care to you, and includes your baby too.

The Short Answer

The Mother & Baby Bonding Package is 120 minutes: 90 minutes of postpartum massage or lymphatic drainage for mom, then 30 minutes of therapist-guided infant massage. It is $400 at home (travel included within about 60 km of Mountain View) or $300 in our Mountain View studio. Packages: 15 visits at $380 or 20 visits at $350 per visit.

What Happens During the Visit

90 min · Mom
Postpartum massage or lymphatic drainage. Side-lying and bolstered positions for comfort. We focus on the neck, shoulders and upper back from feeding, the lower back and hips, and gentle lymphatic drainage for leg and foot swelling. Pressure is always your choice.
30 min · Baby
Therapist-guided infant massage. Your therapist works gently with your baby and shows you simple strokes for the legs, tummy, back and hands that you can repeat at home. You stay with your baby the whole time.

We work around feeds, naps and diaper changes — babies set the schedule, and that is fine. A partner or grandparent is welcome to hold the baby during your 90 minutes, or the baby can nap nearby.

When Can You Start?

  • After a vaginal birth: many mothers book within the first few weeks, once they feel comfortable and their doctor or midwife has no concerns.
  • After a C-section: please wait for clearance from your OB. We avoid the incision area and use gentle side-lying positions; post-surgical lymphatic drainage around the abdomen usually starts later, once your doctor approves.
  • For your baby: please check with your pediatrician before your baby's first massage. We schedule infant massage when your baby is fed, comfortable and awake.

Why New Families Like It

  • No car seat, no travel. We bring the table, linens and unscented, baby-safe oil.
  • Time for you. Ninety minutes of care focused only on your body.
  • A skill you keep. Infant massage is a calm, close routine many parents use before naps or bedtime.
  • A thoughtful gift. Many packages are bought by partners, grandparents or friends — see gift cards.

Who Comes to Your Home

Sessions are performed by Tina Zhang, a California Certified Massage Therapist trained in manual lymphatic drainage through Klose Training, or a California-licensed therapist on her team. Postpartum sessions are usually with a female therapist unless you request otherwise.

Check With Your Doctor

Contact your doctor before booking if you have a fever, signs of infection, heavy bleeding, a suspected blood clot (pain, warmth or swelling in one leg), or high blood pressure after birth. Massage supports recovery and relaxation but is not a substitute for postpartum medical care.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Mother & Baby Bonding Package?

A 120-minute session for a new mother and her baby: 90 minutes of postpartum massage or lymphatic drainage for mom, followed by 30 minutes of therapist-guided infant massage, where the therapist works with the baby and teaches the parent simple techniques to use at home.

When can I get a massage after a C-section?

After clearance from your OB. We avoid the incision area and use gentle side-lying positions; abdominal lymphatic drainage starts only once your doctor approves.

Is infant massage safe for my baby?

Infant massage uses very gentle strokes and is scheduled when your baby is fed, comfortable and awake. Please check with your pediatrician before your baby's first session. A parent stays with the baby the whole time.
