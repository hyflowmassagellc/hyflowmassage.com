Post-Surgery Recovery

How Many Lymphatic Drainage Sessions
Do You Need After Lipo or BBL?

By Tina Zhang, CMT  ·  October 2026  ·  6 min read

"How many sessions will I need?" is the first question almost every liposuction and BBL client asks us. The honest answer depends on your procedure, how much area was treated and how your body heals — but there is a typical range, and knowing it helps you plan your recovery, your schedule and your budget before surgery day.

The Short Answer

Most liposuction and BBL clients do best with a series of about 12–20 manual lymphatic drainage (MLD) sessions over the first 6–8 weeks. Smaller, single-area procedures often need fewer; Lipo 360 and combined procedures usually sit at the higher end. Your surgeon's plan always comes first.

A Typical Week-by-Week Schedule

Sessions are most frequent in the first month, when swelling is highest, then taper as the tissue softens.

Weeks 1–4
2–3 sessions per week (about 8–12 sessions). Start once your surgeon gives written clearance — often within the first week for liposuction and BBL. This is when MLD makes the biggest difference to swelling and helps prevent hard, lumpy areas (fibrosis).
Weeks 5–8
1–2 sessions per week (about 4–8 sessions). Swelling is going down and tissue is softening. Sessions focus on remaining firm or uneven areas.
Month 3+
As needed. Some clients stop here; others book occasional sessions for lingering firmness or fibrosis.

How Your Procedure Changes the Number

ProcedureTypical series
Liposuction, one small area (chin, arms, flanks)Often toward the lower end, around 6–10 sessions
Lipo 360 or BBLUsually 15–20 sessions
Tummy tuck or mommy makeoverUsually 12–20 sessions, starting after surgeon clearance

These are general ranges, not a prescription. After a BBL, we work on the liposuction areas and avoid pressure on the grafted buttocks unless your surgeon says otherwise. If your surgeon recommends a specific number of sessions, we follow that.

Signs You May Need More Sessions

  • Firm, ropy or lumpy areas under the skin that are not softening
  • Swelling that comes back quickly between sessions
  • Uneven swelling from one side to the other
  • Tightness that limits comfortable movement

Signs you can start tapering: swelling is clearly lower, the skin and tissue feel soft, and results hold from one session to the next.

Call Your Surgeon First

Lymphatic drainage is not a substitute for medical care. Contact your surgeon right away for fever, spreading redness or heat, drainage from an incision, sudden swelling or pain in one leg, or new pain that is getting worse. Call 911 for chest pain or trouble breathing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many lymphatic drainage sessions do you need after liposuction?

Most liposuction clients do best with a series of about 12–20 sessions over the first 6–8 weeks: 2–3 sessions a week in weeks 1–4, then 1–2 a week in weeks 5–8. Smaller single-area liposuction often needs fewer; Lipo 360 usually sits at the higher end. Always follow your surgeon's plan.

How many lymphatic massages do you need after a BBL?

A BBL includes liposuction, so most clients follow the same plan of roughly 12–20 sessions over 6–8 weeks. Your therapist works on the liposuction areas and avoids pressure on the grafted buttocks unless your surgeon says otherwise.

When should I stop lymphatic drainage after surgery?

Most clients taper to one session a week once swelling is clearly going down and the tissue feels soft, then stop or move to occasional maintenance. If you still have firm areas or uneven swelling after 8 weeks, a few more sessions can help — check with your surgeon.
