FAQ

Couples Massage at Home: Questions

Do you offer couples massage at home?
Yes. Two therapists come to your home with two massage tables and treat both of you side by side at the same time, anywhere in our Bay Area service area.
How much is a couples massage at home?
$600 for 90 minutes or $800 for 120 minutes for two people, travel included. Packages: 15 sessions at $570 (90 min) or $760 (120 min) each; 20 sessions at $525 (90 min) or $700 (120 min) each.
How much space do we need?
A room of about 10 × 12 ft for two tables side by side. We can also set up in two adjoining rooms if needed.
Can we choose different massages?
Yes. Each person can choose Swedish, deep tissue, therapeutic or lymphatic drainage and their own pressure.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve the Bay Area within 60 km of our Mountain View studio, including Mountain View, Palo Alto, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Los Altos, Santa Clara, San Jose, Saratoga, Campbell, Los Gatos, Menlo Park, Atherton, Redwood City, San Mateo, Fremont and nearby cities. Travel is included in the price.
