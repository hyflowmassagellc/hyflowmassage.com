FAQ

Corporate Chair Massage: Questions

How much does corporate chair massage cost?
On-site chair massage is $150 per therapist hour with a 4-hour minimum per booking ($600), travel included within our Bay Area service area. Sessions are usually 15 minutes per employee.
Is there a minimum booking?
Yes. Each corporate chair massage booking is a minimum of 4 hours per therapist ($600), which covers about 14–16 employees at 15 minutes each.
How many employees can you see in a day?
About four employees per hour at 15 minutes each. For example, 20 employees take 5 hours of massage time — about 5.5 hours including setup and changeovers, or roughly $825.
What do you need from us?
A quiet corner or small room about 6 × 8 ft, close to a power outlet if possible, and a sign-up list. We bring the chair and all supplies.
Do employees need to undress?
No. Chair massage is done fully clothed and without oils, so employees can return straight to work.
Who will my therapist be?
Sessions are delivered by Tina Zhang, HyFlow's owner and lead therapist, or a California-licensed therapist on her team. Female and male therapists are available — just tell us your preference when you book.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve the Bay Area from our Mountain View studio, including Mountain View, Palo Alto, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Los Altos, Santa Clara, San Jose, Saratoga, Campbell, Los Gatos, Menlo Park, Atherton, Redwood City, San Mateo, Fremont, San Francisco, Santa Cruz and nearby cities — travel included. Extended areas within about 2 hours have a travel fee per therapist per visit: +$50 for 37–62 miles (Oakland, Berkeley, Walnut Creek, Pleasanton, Half Moon Bay, Gilroy) and +$80 for 62–100 miles (Carmel, Monterey, Marin, Napa).
Can we start with a pilot event?
Yes. Many companies start with a single 4-hour chair massage event ($600) with no contract, then move to a monthly program if the team enjoys it.
Can you provide a certificate of insurance (COI) for vendor approval?
Yes. We provide a certificate of insurance on request and can complete your company's standard vendor setup forms — mention it in your inquiry and we will send what you need with your quote.
How do invoicing and payment work?
We invoice your company directly. You can pay by corporate card or ACH, and Net-30 payment terms are available.
Are gift card amounts the same as corporate prices?
No. Gift cards are a separate product. Corporate pricing is $150 per therapist hour, 4–7 hours per visit ($600–$1,050), with recurring programs from $600 per month or $7,200 per year.
Can we book corporate chair massage online?
Yes. Choose a 4, 5, 6 or 7-hour visit on our booking page and pay online, or request a quote if you need an invoice first. After you book, we confirm the address, parking, room and sign-up details with you by email or text.
How far in advance should we book?
We recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead, and earlier for December holiday events and January wellness weeks, which fill quickly. Shorter notice is often possible — text (408) 668-6562 to check availability.
Can you bring more than one therapist for a larger team?
Yes. You can book one, two or three licensed therapists for 4 to 7 hours at the same rate of $150 per therapist hour — for example, two therapists for six hours ($1,800) see about 44–48 employees at 15 minutes each, and three therapists for seven hours ($3,150) see about 78–84. For more than three therapists, text (408) 668-6562 and we will plan the schedule with you.
How do employees sign up for a time slot?
We send you a simple sign-up sheet with 15- or 30-minute slots that you can share by email, Slack or a printed sheet in the break room. We keep the schedule moving on the day so everyone gets their full time.
